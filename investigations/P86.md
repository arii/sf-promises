# Evidence Investigation: P86 (Potrero Power Station Mixed-Use Development)

**Promise ID:** P86  
**Display Number:** #32  
**Entity:** Associate Capital / SF Board of Supervisors  
**Sector / Category:** Housing & Neighborhoods  
**Neighborhood:** Pier 70 & Dogpatch (Potrero Power Station)  

## 1. Primary Sources & Evidence Search
- **Origin Target:** Approval and groundbreaking for the 29-acre Potrero Power Station redevelopment.
- **Source Cited:** `http://sfbos.archive.sf.gov/sites/default/files/2025_10_15_PRESS_RELEASE_INNOVATIVE_SOPHIE_MAXWELL_BUILDING_OPENS_AT_SFS_BOOMING_POWER_STATION.pdf`
- **Published Date:** 2020-04 (with 2025 milestone press release).

## 2. Extraction & Temporal Verification
- **Verbatim Quote:** *“The plan calls for more than 2,600 homes...”*
- **Audit Findings:**
  - Originally mislabeled as "Treasure Island Redevelopment" in early drafts.
  - The source is explicitly the Potrero Power Station development along San Francisco's central waterfront (Dogpatch).
  - First affordable residential building (Sophie Maxwell Building, 105 units) delivered and opened in October 2025.

## 3. Advocate Arguments
- **Kept Advocate:** Staged verified opening of Sophie Maxwell Building (Oct 2025) confirming housing delivery under the plan.
- **Broken Advocate:** Notes full 2,600 units are slated across multiple phases through 2035.

## 4. Arbitration Decision
- **Verdict:** `in_progress` (Sophie Maxwell Building delivered; remainder of 2,600-unit master plan under active construction).
- **Neighborhood Correction:** Corrected from erroneous "Treasure Island Redevelopment" to "Pier 70 & Dogpatch (Potrero Power Station)".
