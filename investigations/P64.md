# Evidence Investigation: P64 (Parkmerced Housing Transformation)

**Promise ID:** P64  
**Display Number:** #9  
**Entity:** San Francisco Board of Supervisors / Parkmerced Investors  
**Sector / Category:** Housing & Neighborhoods  
**Neighborhood:** Parkmerced  

## 1. Primary Sources & Evidence Search
- **Origin Target:** SF Board of Supervisors Resolution No. 0241-11 approving the Parkmerced Development Agreement.
- **Source Cited:** `https://sfbos.org/ftp/uploadedfiles/bdsupvrs/resolutions11/r0241-11.pdf`
- **Published Date:** 2011-06-07.

## 2. Extraction & Temporal Verification
- **Flagged Quote:** *“the Project is anticipated to include (1) up to 8,000 new residential units...”*
- **Audit Findings:**
  - *"anticipated to include"* is statutory planning / forecast language, not an unconditional commitment.
  - Development agreements establish project entitlements and limits rather than municipal guarantees.
  - Phase 1 construction suffered repeated legal, financing, and ownership delays through 2026.

## 3. Advocate Arguments
- **Kept Advocate:** Development agreement remains legally in force.
- **Broken Advocate:** 8,000 units are not delivered; project remains stalled in preliminary stages.

## 4. Arbitration Decision
- **Verdict:** `in_progress` (Long-term multi-decade phased development entitlement; not a short-term single-deadline promise).
- **Taxonomy / Neighborhood:** Verified as `Parkmerced` (eliminated earlier erroneous cross-reference).
