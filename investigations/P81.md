# Evidence Investigation: P81 (CPUC Commercial Robotaxi Operating Permits)

**Promise ID:** P81  
**Display Number:** #44  
**Entity:** California Public Utilities Commission (CPUC)  
**Sector / Category:** Transit & Streets  
**Neighborhood:** Commercial Robotaxi Operating Zones  

## 1. Primary Sources & Evidence Search
- **Origin Target:** CPUC regulatory decision approving Cruise LLC and Waymo LLC to charge fares for commercial passenger robotaxi service 24/7 across San Francisco.
- **Source Cited:** `https://cpuc.ca.gov/news-and-updates/all-news/cpuc-approves-permits-for-cruise-and-waymo-to-charge-fares-for-passenger-service-in-sf-2023`
- **Published Date:** 2023-08-10.

## 2. Extraction & Temporal Verification
- **Verbatim Quote:** *“The approval includes the ability for both companies to charge fares for rides at any time of day.”*
- **Audit Findings:**
  - The reviewer noted: *"#39 and #44 are announcements that carry odd verdicts: 'broken' for an approval and a funding announcement."*
  - The CPUC approval itself took effect immediately upon vote on August 10, 2023.
  - However, Cruise's commercial permit was suspended by the California DMV on October 24, 2023 following a pedestrian collision on 5th Street, halting Cruise's operations, while Waymo continued expanding commercial service.
  - Treating a regulatory permit approval as a "broken promise" is an category error under PRD standards. The permit was granted by CPUC. Cruise's individual suspension is a separate company outcome, not a broken CPUC promise.

## 3. Advocate Arguments
- **Kept Advocate:** Regulatory permit was formally issued on August 10, 2023 and remains in effect for authorized commercial providers (Waymo, Zoox).
- **Broken Advocate:** Argued that Cruise's post-launch suspension revoked 24/7 dual-carrier operations.

## 4. Arbitration Decision
- **Verdict:** Reclassified from `broken` to `kept` (The CPUC regulatory action to grant commercial fare approval was fully enacted and executed as stated).
