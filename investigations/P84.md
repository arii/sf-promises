# Evidence Investigation: P84 (Pier 70 / Forest City Housing Approval)

**Promise ID:** P84  
**Display Number:** #19  
**Entity:** Forest City Realty Trust / San Francisco Board of Supervisors  
**Sector / Category:** Housing & Neighborhoods  
**Neighborhood:** Pier 70 & Dogpatch  

## 1. Primary Sources & Evidence Search
- **Origin Target:** SF Board of Supervisors unanimous approval of the 28-acre Pier 70 mixed-use development project.
- **Source Cited:** `https://bizjournals.com/sanfrancisco/news/2017/10/31/forest-city-pier-70-san-francisco-approval-housing.html`
- **Published Date:** 2017-10-31.

## 2. Extraction & Temporal Verification
- **Verbatim Quote:** *“After about a decade of planning, developer Forest City Realty Trust Inc. (NYSE: FCE.A) finally scored city approval... to build up to 2.2 million square feet of offices and up to 3,000 residential units.”*
- **Audit Findings:**
  - Quote reports an approval event ("scored city approval"), but establishes an entitlement ceiling ("up to 3,000 units") rather than a fixed deadline promise.
  - Phase 1 infrastructure and historic building rehabilitation completed, but vertical residential construction paused during the pandemic.

## 3. Advocate Arguments
- **Kept Advocate:** Cites Phase 1 completion, public open space delivery (Building 12), and active commercial leasing.
- **Broken Advocate:** Cites delayed residential towers and paused vertical housing buildout.

## 4. Arbitration Decision
- **Verdict:** `in_progress` (Phased master development actively delivered infrastructure, with remaining residential phases pending).
- **Neighborhood Correction:** Corrected from mislabeled "5M Project (SoMa)" to "Pier 70 & Dogpatch".
