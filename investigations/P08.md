# Evidence Investigation: P08 (Mayor Breed $5M SRO Repair Fund)

**Promise ID:** P08  
**Display Number:** #39  
**Entity:** Mayor London Breed  
**Sector / Category:** Housing & Neighborhoods  
**Neighborhood:** Tenderloin & SoMa (SRO Housing)  

## 1. Primary Sources & Evidence Search
- **Origin Target:** Mayor London Breed May 2022 announcement of $5 million for repairs to San Francisco SRO buildings.
- **Source Cited:** `https://www.sfchronicle.com/sf/article/homeless-housing-sro-breed-17767837.php`
- **Published Date:** 2023-02-07 (Reviewing the May 2022 announcement).

## 2. Extraction & Temporal Verification
- **Flagged Quote:** *“When Mayor London Breed announced $5 million last year for repairs to San Francisco’s crumbling housing...”*
- **Audit Findings:**
  - SF Chronicle reported in February 2023 that nine months after the announcement, zero dollars had been spent or distributed to landlords due to bureaucratic contracting delays and stringent program rules.
  - The funding announcement set expectations for immediate rehabilitation assistance, but failed execution resulted in zero delivered repairs within the expected operating cycle.

## 3. Advocate Arguments
- **Kept Advocate:** Funding was appropriated in the municipal budget.
- **Broken Advocate:** Confirmed zero dollars disbursed and zero repair projects completed after nearly a year; program failed to deliver intended improvements.

## 4. Arbitration Decision
- **Verdict:** `broken` (Funding announcement unfulfilled on delivery timeline; zero repairs initiated during the commitment window).
